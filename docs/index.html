About the project

Trees outside forests (TOF) are the windbreaks, riparian strips, farmstead shelterbelts and scattered trees that national forest inventories do not count, yet which matter for carbon, shelter and habitat across the northern Great Plains. This project detects them from high-resolution NAIP aerial imagery and estimates how much of the landscape they cover.

Because we cannot classify every image, we work from a sample. LRR F is divided into its 11 Major Land Resource Areas (MLRAs), and within each MLRA a set of 1 km grid cells is drawn for imagery acquisition and classification. The maps on this page show that sample and the ground-truth sites that anchor it.

The workflow lives in the treesOutsideForests repository: annual forest and urban masks from NLCD and the Census, NAIP acquisition and segmentation for the sampled cells, and the sample design shown here.

How to read the maps

  • Training site: a ground-truth 1 km cell used to fit the model.
  • Validation site: a ground-truth cell held back to test the model.
  • Sampled 1 km cell: drawn for NAIP acquisition (outlines appear when zoomed in).
  • MLRA fill: pale colour per Major Land Resource Area; hover for its name.
  • Census places 2020: incorporated places, hidden until switched on.
  • NLCD forest 2020: share of each block that is forest, hidden until switched on.

Each interactive map has grey and satellite base maps, a layer switcher, and hover labels giving the cell id and MLRA. The print version is the same map as a single PNG.
